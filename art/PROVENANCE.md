# Art provenance

Generated with Higgsfield, image model `gpt_image_2_6`, quality high, 2K, transparent background, in November 2026.

- A first sheet of eight flat editorial vector characters (designer, researcher, engineer, content strategist, product manager, operations lead, director, and a small rounded agent icon) set the style. Later sheets used it as the style reference.
- Two more sheets of five people each, chosen for range of age, body size and background.
- Portrait grids of invented people, each grid prompt naming 25 specific invented people. Near-duplicates were removed by image similarity.

`cut/body-NN.webp` are full figures; `cut/face-NN.webp` and `cut/face-uNNN.webp` are shoulders-up crops masked to a circle; `cut/agent.webp` is the agent icon. Figures were cut from the sheets with a hard alpha matte (alpha at least 200, 1px eroded, 0.8px feather), cropped and resized.

No figure depicts a real person, and no photographs were used as references.
